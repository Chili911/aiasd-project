\# Privacy Policy for Weatherly



Last updated: 2026-11-21



\## Overview

Weatherly is a weather forecasting and avatar outfit-styling application developed as part of a university software development project.



\## Weather \& App Data

Weatherly retrieves weather forecast information and displays weather-based recommendations (such as outfits, activities, and places) and customizable avatar styling options.
