**Assistant used and what I gave it**

Assistant used was ChatGPT. I gave it my PROPOSAL.md Part B (§8–§12) as it stood and asked: “You are an investor deciding whether to fund this. If you were an investor, what are the three main reasons you would not invest in this project? Please give specific reasons based on our proposal, not general reasons.


**The three objections**

1. The market evidence is too weak because the initial validation only involves 20 students, and there is not yet enough evidence about the wider market.

2. Weatherly’s advantage over existing weather apps is not clearly proven. It combines weather, outfit, activity, and place recommendations, but the proposal does not yet show that users would prefer this over existing alternatives.

3. The project has several technical risks, including dependence on a weather API, device compatibility, free hosting limits, and possible delays with Galaxy Store publication.


**The one that was right — what I changed in the proposal**

**§8 – Market and target users:** I agreed that the market evidence was too weak. I made the 20-student survey more specific by stating what we will ask students about their weather-checking habits, outfit and activity searches, and interest in Weatherly’s recommendations. I also added that a named public source will be used to estimate the wider market.

**Change log:** 2026-10-03 — Strengthened §8 by adding specific questions for the 20-student survey and a plan to support the wider market estimate with a named public source.

**The one that was wrong — and how I know**

The objection that Weatherly has no clear advantage over existing weather apps was wrong. I checked AccuWeather’s current features and found that it provides weather forecasts and alerts, but it does not combine these with personalized outfit, activity, and place recommendations. Weatherly’s proposal includes all of these features together in §9 and §10. Therefore, the competitor objection is not supported by the feature comparison I checked.
